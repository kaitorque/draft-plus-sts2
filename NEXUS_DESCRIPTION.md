[center][size=5][b]Draft+[/b][/size][/center]
[i]Starter (Basic) cards for Draft / Sealed Deck / Insanity Custom mode—plus optional rewards & shops modifiers.[/i]

[line]

[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]

[b]Draft+[/b] adds a companion modifier called [b]Starter+[/b]. Pick it together with [b]Draft[/b], [b]Sealed Deck[/b], or [b]Insanity[/b] to let [b]starter (Basic) cards[/b] show up in that mode's Neow card flow.

With [b]Character Cards[/b] enabled, each extra character you turn on has its [b]starting deck[/b] merged into the shared starter set used by [b]Draft+[/b], [b]Starter Rewards[/b], and [b]Starter Shops[/b]—so those starters can show up in the draft pool (and in rewards/shops if those modifiers are on).

Beyond Neow, the mod adds two optional toggles: [b]Starter Rewards[/b] lets starter cards appear in combat card rewards, and [b]Starter Shops[/b] lets starter cards appear in merchant offers under the mod’s shop rules. Turn on only what you want; nothing forces rewards or shops if you only care about the draft.

[b]Minus Strike & Defend[/b] (optional): if you use [b]Starter+[/b], [b]Starter Rewards[/b], or [b]Starter Shops[/b], this removes [b]Basic Strike/Defend[/b] cards from those eligible pools.

[b]Pandora’s Box[/b]: With Draft / Sealed Deck / Insanity enabled, Pandora’s Box cannot be offered by [b]Darv[/b]. With this mod, it can be offered only if your deck still has at least one [b]Strike[/b] or [b]Defend[/b].
